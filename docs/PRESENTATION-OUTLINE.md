| 7 | **AI analytics approach** | Decode (PTS-driven, TCP, backoff) → YOLO-v9-t plate detector (ONNX) → PaddleOCR two-line aware → position-aware normalisation (20 test vectors) → char-wise voting → sightings; YOLOX-s vehicle/person counts; quality section with spot-check accuracy; intrusion (P2) and FRS (roadmap) | `04-camera-live-reads.png` (camera page with live reads + object counts) | "Plates are read at 5 fps on road-facing cameras and at keyframe rate on all fifty, so the whole 12-hour loop is indexed. | 8 | **Watchlist correlation & alerting — the four boxes** | **Database structure** (watchlist, plate_reads, sightings, alerts, events, audit) · **Matching logic** (normalise → exact → Levenshtein-1 'possible' at conf ≥ 0.8 → suppression 60 s → priority = max(entry, reason floor) − 1 if possible) · **Alerting mechanism** (WS envelope, desktop notification, webhook HMAC, Telegram P2, escalation > 5 min) · **User interface** (toast, panel, drawer with crop/recording/clip, route) | `diagrams/06-integration-workflow.svg` (full slide) | "The portal asks for the complete integration workflow on one slide — here it is: the tables, the matching rules, the channels and the screens. | 10 | **Existing departmental systems remain unaffected** (Model 2 note) | Direct read-only RTSP/ONVIF; exactly one connection per camera via the internal relay; nothing installed at the department; no middleware/federation (FAQ Q17); reversible; failure-isolated | Simple diagram: department NVR → single arrow → relay → all consumers | "This is the Model 2 architecture note.
